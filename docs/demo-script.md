# 3-minute demo outline

**0:00–0:20 — Hook**
“Generative AI creates first and looks for value later. We reversed that. CREATIVE//BOUNTY finds paid demand first.” Show €0 external capital and Opportunity Radar.

**0:20–0:55 — Rights gate**
Open three briefs: one PASS, one REVIEW because AI permission is unclear, one REJECT because AI is prohibited. Stress fail-closed behavior.

**0:55–1:25 — Economic decision**
Open the permitted opportunity. Show advertised reward, deterministic economic score, zero fabricated win probability, and hard budget rule.

**1:25–2:15 — Generation loop**
Pursue. Show attempt 1 rejected, feedback, retry/fallback, attempt 2 accepted. In final LIVE demo this must be a real Genblaze execution.

**2:15–2:40 — B2 evidence**
Show B2 objects: source brief, rights decision, economics, rejected and accepted assets, evaluation, Genblaze manifest hash.

**2:40–3:00 — Close**
“We don’t generate media and hope somebody buys it. We generate only when demand exists, AI is allowed, and the economics pass. Demand before generation.”
