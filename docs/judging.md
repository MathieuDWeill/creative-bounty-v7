# Judging map

## Real-World Utility
Turns observable paid demand into a controlled creative-production workflow. Avoids speculative generation and blocks policy-incompatible work.

## Production Readiness
Fail-closed rights gate, budget invariant, append-only ledger, deterministic scoring, retriable pipeline design, explicit SAMPLE/LIVE distinction.

## B2 Storage and Data Orchestration
B2 is the audit/evidence layer, storing source brief, policy evidence, decisions, every generation attempt, accepted asset, manifests and outcome.

## Genblaze
LIVE design uses the official Pipeline abstraction, provider swapping/fallback, agent-loop evaluation and B2-compatible ObjectStorageSink. The final submission should demonstrate at least one real provider call and real Genblaze manifest.
